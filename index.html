The Problem

India's healthcare runs on compliance. Compliance still runs on paper.

Every ultrasound centre files Form F by hand. Every radiology department tracks AERB licences in spreadsheets. Every hospital prepares for NABH audits with last-minute folder-stuffing. At 5 branches, this is stressful. At 150+, it's a systemic risk.

One missed sequence number during an inspection. One expired radiation safety certificate. One incomplete audit trail. The penalties aren't warnings — they're sealed machines, cancelled licences, and legal notices.

Healthcare regulation in India needs infrastructure, not more checklists. That's what Samhitha builds — the compliance rails that every hospital chain can run on.

PCPNDT Compliance, automated end-to-end

Built by a practising radiologist who fills Form F every day. Not by a software team guessing at clinical workflows.

01

Auto-Populated Form F

The majority of form fields are auto-populated from your existing HIS/EHR — no re-entry, no transcription errors, no manual copying between systems.

02

Tamper-Proof Sequencing

Automatic sequential numbering across every branch. No gaps, no duplicates, no manual registers — even at 500+ entries per day per centre.

03

Inspection Readiness

Real-time compliance scores per branch. Generate inspection-ready registers and reports in seconds. Know your status before the Appropriate Authority walks in.

04

Network-Wide Command Centre

One dashboard for your entire chain. Instantly flag non-compliant branches, track form accuracy, monitor sequence integrity, and drill down into any centre's compliance history.
