The Compliance Gap

One missed sequence number.
One unsealed register.
One failed inspection.

PCPNDT non-compliance doesn't just mean paperwork errors — it means sealed ultrasound machines, legal notices, and reputational damage that takes years to recover from. At scale, the risk multiplies with every branch.

📋

Form F Errors

Manual data entry across hundreds of scans per day leads to missing fields, wrong sequences, and incomplete declarations — each one a potential violation.

🔢

Sequence Breaks

500+ entries per day at high-volume branches. One jumbled sequence number during a surprise inspection and the entire register is questioned.

⚖️

Legal Exposure

The PCPNDT Act carries penalties including imprisonment, equipment seizure, and licence cancellation. The Appropriate Authority doesn't send warnings — they send notices.

What Samhitha Does

Compliance on autopilot

Built by a radiologist who fills Form F every day — not by a software team guessing at workflows.

📝

Auto-Fill Form F

Patient demographics, referring doctor details, and centre information populate automatically from your existing HIS/EHR. The patient only signs the declaration.

🔗

Sequence Management

Automatic, tamper-proof sequential numbering across all branches. No gaps, no duplicates, no manual tracking — even at 500+ entries per day.

🔍

Inspection Readiness

Real-time compliance dashboard for every branch. Know your audit status before the Appropriate Authority walks in — not after.

📊

Network-Wide Oversight

One dashboard for your entire chain. Flag non-compliant branches instantly. Generate inspection-ready reports in seconds, not hours.

⚡

Lightweight Integration

Samhitha works alongside your existing systems — no migration, no downtime, no IT overhaul. Deploys in days, not months. Your front desk workflow stays the same; the compliance layer happens automatically.
